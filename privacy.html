Privacy policy

Family Wall Calendar is a private app used by one household, on its own home server.

What it accesses. With the household's permission, it reads and changes events in Google Calendar and tasks in Google Tasks, in the one Google account the household connects.

How it uses that data. Only to show those events and tasks on the household's screen, and to send back what the family adds or changes there.

Where it is stored. A copy, and the sign-in that keeps the connection working, are kept on the household's own server at home.

Who it is shared with. Nobody. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not sent to anyone other than Google.

Removing access. The household can disconnect the app on its Setup screen, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
